Before and after

Old way, new way.

Applicant experience
BeforeApplicants apply blind, with no projects shown.
NowSuitable projects are shown upfront as they apply.
Screening & shortlisting
BeforeOfficers screen and shortlist by hand in Excel.
NowIneligible applicants filtered automatically; the system scores suitability per project.
Candidate 360
BeforeAn applicant's past engagement with DSTA isn't captured.
NowEvery touchpoint in one view: IP, BrainHack, events.
Applicant summary
BeforeIO and mentor dig through the raw CV and form.
NowHighlights surfaced at a glance, like a past BrainHack participant or strong leadership.
Project approval
BeforeProject approvals needed DCE sign off.
NowDCE approval removed from the flow.
Intelligence built in

Faster, clearer, sharper.

Project quality check
Checks each project submission for writing and whether it sits at the right level.
Suitability scoring
Scores every applicant against every project to help the IO shortlist.
Applicant summary
A rich summary of each applicant for the IO and mentor to read at a glance.
Interview evaluation
Pulls the transcript, mentor notes, attribute scores and CV highlights into a summary for the mentor's final decision.
Feedback analysis
Reads intern feedback to build a sentiment analysis across calibration, mentorship and environment.
