B1.1 · Application

Scenario.

An Undergraduate Student applies and selects all 3 internship windows. Each window has its own set of projects.
Jan – Jun 2026
8 projects
May – Aug 2026
6 projects
Jul – Dec 2026
7 projects
Option A
5 projects per window
A ranked set of up to 5 for each window, so up to 15 rankings in total.
or
Option B
5 projects across all windows
One combined ranking of up to 5, spanning the 3 windows.
?Each internship window has its own projects, so this choice shapes both the application and how shortlisting works. To decide.
